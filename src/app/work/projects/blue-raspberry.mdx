## Mission Statement

Our mission with this film is to tell a grounded, authentic coming-of-age story that focuses on vulnerability and self-expression. We want to make a final product that helps girls and young women to feel more comfortable just as they are, and not feel the need to hide any parts of them.

## The Story

Blue Raspberry is a story about authenticity, vulnerability, and identity.

![text reading "Why this story?"](/images/projects/blue-raspberry/why-this-story.webp)

Being a woman is complicated. You are constantly expected to act, look, and dress a certain way to be seen as valid. Countless depictions of women in the media only show the romanticized and filtered parts of femininity, when in reality, womanhood is much more complicated. We deal with these societal pressures daily, and the most raw, authentic parts of us are often censored.

In Blue Raspberry, Avery tries to give herself a makeover to impress her friend, Jordan, whom she also has feelings for. In the end, she learns that Jordan likes her just as she is, and reminds her that she should never have to try and change herself to impress anyone else.

Blue Raspberry's goal is to show all of these aspects of femininity that the media tries to hide, in order to help make girls and young women more comfortable in their skin. For the creative aspect of the film, we want to incorporate colors that represent each character, and focus on pink, purple, and blue tones and an early 2000s vibe. See our mood board below for visuals that will inspire the creative direction of the project.

![Moodboard with hues of blue/pink/purple and skater/grune/grime aesthetic](/images/projects/blue-raspberry/moodboard.webp)

You're probably wondering, where does blue raspberry as a flavor come into play? Good question. When you think about it, all artificial flavors are made (or supposed to be) from the fruit it comes from. Grape slushies come from grapes, cherry slushies come from cherries; however, blue raspberries don't exist in nature. Therefore, they do not need to conform to any preset expectations or societal norms. Sound familiar? 😉

![text reading "Period Kits Ohio"](/images/projects/blue-raspberry/period-kits-ohio.webp)

Blue Raspberry is a film that centers around unfiltered, messy aspects of being a woman. An example of this is unexpected periods, as featured at one point in the film. We believe that access to period products is a human right; however, dozens of people across the United States and the world are still unable to afford or gain access to these resources. This is why we will be making personal donations to Period Kits Ohio, as 10% of our earnings will go towards them.

Period Kits Ohio is an organization that aims to fight poverty by donating 'period kits,' consisting of any and all feminine products that one might need. They donate to underprivileged, less fortunate individuals and families that are unable to comfortably afford period products. They strongly believe that access to menstrual products is a human right, and no one should ever have to choose between buying food or pads, for example. You can check out their website here and check out their [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/periodkitsoh/) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/periodkitsoh) for more information.

![Period Kits Ohio logo](/images/projects/blue-raspberry/period-kits-logo.webp)

Geoff, the founder of the company, started the organization 8 years ago in Denver, Colorado. He had volunteer experience helping homeless people in the area and learned firsthand how these individuals manage periods with limited resources. He then decided to found Period Kits Ohio, eventually donating 5 million period products to non-profits across 4 U.S. states. He also strongly believes that the stigma surrounding periods is destructive and unnecessary, and believes that people should be able to talk freely about and be comfortable with the idea of menstruation. De-stigmatizing concepts that are natural parts of womanhood is a core concept in our film, which is why we decided to donate to this organization specifically.
